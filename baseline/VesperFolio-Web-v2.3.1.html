How it works & privacy

Add pictures, arrange pages, then export. Crop uses the edges of the rotated image. “Document” boosts contrast; “Grey” removes colour. Edits never change your original files.

Word documents contain pictures, not editable or searchable text. To use Google Docs, upload the exported .docx in Drive and open it with Google Docs.

This app has no network requests, accounts or analytics. Pictures are held only for this session: export before closing or rotating an APK that reloads on rotation. Up to 24 pages; imported pictures are resized to 2200 pixels on their longest edge to control memory use. Animated pictures use a single frame. A 24-million-pixel session budget can limit imports before 24 pages. Undo history is trimmed when retained images exceed 32 million pixels. Actual browser memory use is higher and device dependent.

Privacy, terms & disclaimer

Privacy

This file processes images locally without uploads, analytics, cookies, accounts or network libraries. Pages and undo history are held in memory, not automatically saved. Clear removes pages from view; Undo can restore them. Discard session removes pages and Undo history from app memory references. Close the page to end the session. This is not a secure erasure guarantee.

Exported files remain wherever you save them. Printing may send pages to an operating system print service, network printer or cloud provider you select. A host serving this web-app may log visits; browser extensions and APK wrappers have their own behaviour and policies.

Terms

Use images you are authorised to process. You control what you import, export and share. No account or payment is required by this web-app.
